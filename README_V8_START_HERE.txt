Pulse Fitness SmartQuote V8 - Upgrade and quick check
======================================================
1. CLOSE the old V7 command window (otherwise Windows may continue showing V7).
2. Back up your V7 folder including smartquote_local.db.
3. Extract V8 ZIP into a new folder.
4. Copy V7 smartquote_local.db into the new V8 app folder before launching
   if you wish to keep old user accounts and quote history.
5. Double-click start_windows.bat and sign in as normal.
6. Open your existing Skipton draft: V8 upgrades previously unpriced
   family-code rows automatically without having to reupload the agreement.
7. A NEW upload of the same agreement gives 31 rows, 55 machine units,
   29 priced lines (mostly INDICATIVE FAMILY ESTIMATES), 2 still TBC.
8. Check the original agreement's fixed-price extension at £14,212.43
   per year before proposing an alternate customer price.

Historic-family rates reference modern catalogue RRPs; they do NOT prove
that an old SKU/console has a like-for-like current replacement. The two
remaining unpriced codes are 270G-6.0 and WattBPro. If you have verified
historic prices for them, use Master Price List (manager/admin) or enter
an evidenced annual unit rate in Edit line.

This is an internal pilot, not approved for unrestricted customer quoting.
See V8_RELEASE_NOTES.md for detail.
