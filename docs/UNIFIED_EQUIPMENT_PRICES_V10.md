# V10 unified Equipment & Prices — 21 October 2026

The two sidebar menu entries ("Equipment Catalogue" and "Master Price List") are now one visible **Equipment & Prices** destination.

## Coordinator workflow
- Search by code, model, or name. Current catalogue, historical matches and 2026 RRP source evidence remain available.
- View details shows the complete underlying master product record (all stored fields), resolved pricing configuration, service rate values, console rules, source/date and Silver, Gold, Platinum annual/monthly estimates.
- Select multiple SKUs via checkboxes; selection persists across searches. Print selection creates an A4 landscape print sheet, including Save as PDF, with current master RRP, console, monthly and annual prices for all three tiers. At most 75 SKUs per batch.
- Missing RRPs and conflicting SKUs show a dash rather than a made-up price; customer invoice sales prices are never treated as RRPs.

## Manager controls preserved
The manager/admin sees a Manage master prices tab inside the same feature. The existing edit/create, export, price-list preview/apply, RRP reconciliation, legacy exceptions, matching memory, missing-equipment report and audit tools remain intact and protected by the original backend roles. Historical archive/commercial diagnostics remain accessible inside a closed admin-only disclosure, no longer displayed as a prominent warning to staff.

## Pricing basis
The printable values are produced by the existing price_line tariff from the currently loaded managed catalogue at 0% discount, ex VAT, annual and monthly per machine. Source and provisional pricing status are printed. These internal guide values do not constitute tariff approval or an issued customer quotation. Issued PDFs, quote history, source masters and underlying data are unchanged.

Verify one staff navigation destination, exact detailed source fields, cross-search selection, correct three-tier prices, invalid selection handling, preserved manager permissions, preserved manager editing/imports, Windows startup, Windows portable build and application regressions.
